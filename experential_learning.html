Facilities & Services

Our hospital can handle over 1200 outpatients and 900 inpatients daily, offering cutting-edge treatments and holistic care.

Advanced Operation Theatres

15 modular operation theatres with laminar airflow and modern surgical equipment.

State-of-the-Art ICUs

SICU, MICU, RICU, NICU & PICU with emergency backups at every bedside.

Patient Accommodation

General wards, shared rooms, and private rooms (AC/non-AC) for comfort and care.

Diagnostic Services

Lab, ECG, TMT, ECHO, X-ray, CT Scan, Ultrasound, Doppler & Pulmonary Tests.

Emergency & Trauma Care

24x7 trauma and emergency care with ambulance and post-operative support.

Clinical Excellence

Experienced consultants with excellent success rates in surgical outcomes.

Specialty Diagnostics

Colonoscopy, Mammography, Pap Smear, Audiometry & BERA Tests.

Holistic Healing

Yoga, Music Therapy, Physiotherapy, and dietary counseling services.

24x7 Support Facilities

Blood Bank, Pharmacy, Ambulance Services and emergency support around the clock.

Health Checkup Programs

Master Health Checkups designed for preventive care and wellness monitoring.
