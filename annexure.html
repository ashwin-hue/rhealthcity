Scope of services

onboarding

Welcome to Annai hospital! Our onboarding program is designed to help new staff integrate smoothly into our hospital environment. It includes an orientation on hospital values, patient care standards, and departmental workflows. You’ll receive a tour of key facilities, training on protocols like infection control and documentation, and access to hospital systems and IDs. HR formalities such as payroll, duty rosters, and biometric setup will also be completed. A mentor will guide you during your initial days to ensure a confident start. For any support, please contact the HR or Administration team.

Dependencies

Hospital onboarding involves several essential steps and dependencies to ensure that new staff are fully prepared for their roles. The process begins with the submission of personal and professional documents, including government-issued ID proof, educational and experience certificates, a medical fitness certificate, passport-size photographs, and the official appointment letter. The HR department manages administrative requirements such as generating employee ID cards, completing biometric registration, collecting bank details for salary processing, and recording emergency contact information.Following documentation, the new employee undergoes a comprehensive orientation program that introduces the hospital's mission, vision, clinical protocols, and safety standards. Training is provided on crucial topics like infection control, patient safety, fire safety, and documentation practices. Access is granted to digital tools such as the Hospital Management System (HMS), Electronic Medical Records (EMR), and official email or communication platforms.Departmental integration is another important aspect, where the new staff member is assigned to a specific unit, introduced to their reporting manager and colleagues, and given their duty schedule along with relevant departmental SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures). Lastly, compliance-related formalities such as signing a code of conduct, confidentiality agreement, and submission of valid professional licenses or registrations (for clinical roles) must be completed. These dependencies ensure a smooth transition into the hospital environment and help uphold high standards of care and professionalism.

Specializations

Our hospital offers a wide range of medical and surgical specializations, providing advanced, focused care across multiple disciplines. Each department is staffed by experienced specialists and supported by modern diagnostic and treatment facilities. Key specializations include General Medicine, General Surgery, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Orthopedics, Cardiology, Neurology, Psychiatry, Dermatology, ENT, Ophthalmology, Radiology, Anesthesiology, and Emergency Medicine. These departments not only deliver comprehensive patient care but also play a critical role in clinical training and research for medical students and residents.

Separate Corporate Wing for Outpatient and Inpatient Care

At our hospital, we understand the distinct needs of our corporate clients. That’s why we offer a dedicated corporate wing for both outpatient and inpatient services, designed to provide personalized care, reduced wait times, and a seamless healthcare experience.

Outpatient Services:

Our Corporate Outpatient Wing ensures quick consultations, priority appointments, and a private, comfortable setting for diagnostics and follow-up visits. We offer:

  • Express registration and billing
  • Dedicated consultation rooms
  • Exclusive lounge and waiting areas
  • Health check packages customized for corporate employees
Inpatient Services:

Our Corporate Inpatient Wing features well-appointed rooms and round-the-clock attention to comfort and care. Highlights include:

  • Priority admission and discharge process
  • Executive rooms and suites with premium amenities
  • Personalized case management
  • Liaison support for HR and insurance coordination
